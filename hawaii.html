Day One · Sunday, May 17

Arrival

Wheels down in Kona, drive up to the resort

  • 2:10 PMLand in Kona
  • ~2:30 PMPick up the car — Hertz Jeep Compass AWD
  • ~3:00 PMDrive to Rosewood Kona Village (~20 min)
  • EveningCheck in, explore the resort, decompress
  • DinnerMoana at Kona Village or Beach Tree next door at the Four Seasons

Day Two · Monday, May 18

Resort & Mauna Lani

Black sand in the morning, town in the evening

  • EarlyBreakfast at the resort
  • ~10:00 AMDrive to 49 Black Sand Beach at Mauna Lani (~10 min) — stop at the guard shack for a beach pass
  • ~12:30 PMShops at Mauna Lani — browse, lunch or a drink
  • ~2:30 PMBack to the resort — pool, beach, decompress
  • ~5:00 PMLeave for Kailua-Kona town (~30 min)
  • ~5:30 PMWalk Ali‘i Drive — Hulihee Palace, Mokuaikaua Church, Kailua Pier; sunset around 6:55
  • 7:00 PMDinner at Kona Brewing Company — Kailua-Kona pub
  • ~9:00 PMDrive back to the resort

Day Three · Tuesday, May 19

South Kona & lū‘au

Salt, coffee, and a long evening on the north shore

  • 9:00 AMKona Sea Salt farm tour + mineral water soak — Kawaihae
  • ~10:30 AMWrap up at the salt farm, drive south to Captain Cook (~1 hr)
  • 11:30 AMGreenwell Farms coffee tour — free, walk-in
  • ~12:30 PMLunch in Captain Cook + Kealakekua Bay / Captain Cook Monument lookout
  • ~2:30 PMDrive back to the resort (~45 min) — rest, change
  • ~4:45 PMLeave for Mauna Kea Beach Hotel (~30 min)
  • 5:30 PMLū‘au at Mauna Kea Beach Hotel

Day Four · Wednesday, May 20

Hamakua, Hilo & Mauna Kea

The longest day — coast, waterfalls, and stars at altitude

  • ~8:30 AMLeave for the Hamakua Coast (~1 hr 45 min via Saddle Road)
  • 10:45 AMHawaiian Vanilla Company farm tour — Paauilo
  • ~12:30 PMDrive to Hilo (~45 min), stop at Akaka Falls — easy 0.4-mile loop
  • LunchSuisan Fish Market poke bowls, eat at Lili‘uokalani Gardens
  • ~2:30 PMRainbow Falls (quick stop)
  • ~3:30 PMLeave Hilo, head up Saddle Road toward Mauna Kea (~1 hr)
  • ~4:30 PMArrive at the Mauna Kea Visitor Information Station (9,200 ft)
  • SunsetSunset from the Visitor Center area
  • After darkStargazing at the Visitor Center
  • LateDrive back to the resort (~1.5 hrs)

Day Five · Thursday, May 21

Volcanoes & South Point

Slow start, then the long arc to the southernmost point and the caldera

  • MorningSlow morning at the resort — breakfast, pool, beach
  • 12:30 PMLeave the resort, head south on Hwy 11
  • ~1:45 PMLunch at Hana Hou Restaurant in Na‘alehu — southernmost restaurant in the US
  • 2:30 PMTurn onto South Point access road
  • 2:45 PMSouth Point (Ka Lae) — southernmost point in the US; cliffs, wind-bent trees
  • 3:30 PMBack on Hwy 11, continue to Volcanoes
  • 4:15 PMWelcome Center at Kīlauea Military Camp — park store, shirts, maps
  • 4:45 PMVolcano House for first caldera view
  • 5:15 PMUēkahuna overlook (primary eruption viewpoint), then Crater Rim Drive: Kīlauea Overlook, Steaming Bluff, Waldron Ledge
  • 6:30 PMDinner in Volcano Village — Kīlauea Lodge or Tuk-Tuk Thai
  • 7:45 PMReturn to Uēkahuna for lava glow after dark
  • 8:45 PMBegin the drive back to the resort (~2.5 hrs)
